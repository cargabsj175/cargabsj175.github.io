## Why a blog?

For years I have built websites for clients, agencies, and personal projects. Most of that work lives in repositories, screenshots, and conversations that disappear over time. This blog is my attempt to keep the useful parts: the decisions, the mistakes, and the small discoveries that make a project better.

## What you will find here

- Notes about **web development**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, and modern tooling.
- Practical **Linux** topics: servers, the command line, and self-hosted tools.
- Write-ups about the projects I build and the problems they solve.
